1. Study Notes

What is an equation?

An equation is a mathematical statement showing that two expressions are equal.

Example:

2x + 4 = 12

Our goal is to find the value of x that makes the equation true.

Solving linear equations

A linear equation has the variable raised to the power of 1.

Example:

3x + 5 = 20

Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides.

3x = 15

Step 2: Divide both sides by 3.

x = 5

Quadratic equations

A quadratic equation has the highest power of the variable equal to 2.

ax² + bx + c = 0

Methods of solving include:

  • Factorisation
  • Completing the square
  • Quadratic formula

Quadratic formula:

x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a

What is an inequality?

An inequality compares two expressions that are not necessarily equal.

  • < means less than
  • > means greater than
  • ≤ means less than or equal to
  • ≥ means greater than or equal to

Example:

x + 3 > 7

x > 4

Important inequality rule

When multiplying or dividing both sides of an inequality by a negative number, reverse the inequality sign.

Example:

-2x > 8

x < -4
