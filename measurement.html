Combined Objects

Combined objects are 3D shapes made by joining two or more familiar geometric solids. We use the formulas for individual shapes to calculate the total surface area and volume.

1. What are Combined Objects?

A combined object is a 3D object formed by joining two or more different solids.

Examples include:

  • A cylinder with a hemisphere on top.
  • A cone joined to a cylinder.
  • A rectangular prism with a triangular prism roof.

2. Volume of Combined Objects

Volume is the amount of space occupied by a three-dimensional object.

Volume of combined objects:

Total volume = Volume of first solid + Volume of second solid

If part of an object is removed, subtract the volume of the removed part instead.

3. Surface Area of Combined Objects

Surface area is the total area of the outside surfaces of an object.

Important:

Only count surfaces that are exposed on the outside of the combined object.

When two solids are joined, their touching surfaces are inside the combined object. Do not include those surfaces in the external surface area.

4. Important Volume Formulas

Cylinder:

V = πr²h

Cone:

V = ⅓πr²h

Sphere:

V = ⁴⁄₃πr³

Hemisphere:

V = ⅔πr³

Rectangular prism:

V = length × width × height

5. Important Surface Area Formulas

Cylinder:

Curved surface area = 2πrh

Cone:

Curved surface area = πrl

Sphere:

Surface area = 4πr²

Hemisphere:

Curved surface area = 2πr²

In the cone formula, l represents the slant height.

6. Steps for Solving Combined Object Problems

  1. Identify all the individual 3D shapes.
  2. Write down the measurements provided.
  3. Identify which formula is needed.
  4. Calculate each individual volume or area.
  5. Add or subtract where necessary.
  6. Include the correct units in your final answer.
Remember:

Volume is measured in cubic units, for example cm³ or m³.

Surface area is measured in square units, for example cm² or m².
