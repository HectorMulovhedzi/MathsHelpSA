Exponents

Learn exponent laws, negative exponents and exponential equations.

Worked Example

Simplify:

2³ × 2⁴ ÷ 2²

Step 1: Use the multiplication law.

2³ × 2⁴ = 2³⁺⁴

Step 2: Use the division law.

2⁷ ÷ 2² = 2⁷⁻²

Step 3: Simplify.

2⁵ = 32

Final Answer: 32

Equations

Solve linear, quadratic and exponential equations.

Worked Example

Solve for x:

x² - 5x + 6 = 0

Step 1: Factorise.

(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0

Step 2: Make each bracket equal to zero.

x - 2 = 0 or x - 3 = 0

Step 3: Solve.

x = 2 or x = 3

Final Answer: x = 2 or x = 3

Analytical Geometry

Learn about gradients, distance between points, midpoints, equations of straight lines and circles.

Distance Between Two Points

Worked Example

Find the distance between A(1, 2) and B(4, 6).

Distance formula:

d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]

Step 1: Substitute the coordinates.

d = √[(4 − 1)² + (6 − 2)²]

Step 2: Simplify.

d = √[3² + 4²]

d = √[9 + 16]

Step 3: Calculate.

d = √25

Final answer: 5 units.

Euclidean Geometry

Learn geometric theorems involving circles, chords, tangents, angles and cyclic quadrilaterals.

Cyclic Quadrilateral

Theorem: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180°.

Worked Example

A cyclic quadrilateral has one angle of 80°. Find its opposite angle.

Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180°.

Step 1: Write the theorem.

Opposite angles = 180°

Step 2: Substitute.

x + 80° = 180°

Step 3: Solve.

x = 180° − 80°

Final answer: x = 100°.

∫

Calculus

Learn about differentiation, derivatives, gradients, stationary points and optimisation.

Worked Example

Differentiate the following function:

f(x) = x² + 3x + 2

Step 1: Apply the power rule.

d/dx (x²) = 2x

Step 2: Differentiate the remaining terms.

d/dx (3x) = 3

d/dx (2) = 0

Step 3: Combine the answers.

f'(x) = 2x + 3

Final answer: f'(x) = 2x + 3.
