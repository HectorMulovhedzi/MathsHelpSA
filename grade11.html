Exponents

Learn the laws of exponents and how to simplify expressions.

Worked Example

Simplify:

2³ × 2²

Step 1: Identify the bases.

Both terms have base 2.

Step 2: Apply the multiplication law.

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents.

2³ × 2² = 2³⁺²

Step 3: Add the exponents.

2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2

Final Answer: 32

x

Equations

Learn how to solve equations and find the value of x.

Worked Example

Solve for x:

2x + 4 = 12

Step 1: Subtract 4 from both sides.

2x = 12 - 4

Step 2: Simplify.

2x = 8

Step 3: Divide both sides by 2.

x = 8 ÷ 2

Final Answer: x = 4

Trigonometry

Understand sine, cosine and tangent ratios.

Worked Example

Calculate sin(30°).

sin(30°) = ?

Step 1: Recall the exact trigonometric value.

Step 2: Use the standard special-angle value.

sin(30°) = 1/2

Final Answer: 1/2

%

Probability

Learn how to calculate the probability of events.

Worked Example

A fair die has six sides. What is the probability of rolling a 4?

P(rolling a 4) = ?

Step 1: Count the possible outcomes.

There are 6 possible outcomes.

Step 2: Count the favourable outcomes.

Only one outcome is a 4.

Step 3: Apply the probability formula.

P(A) = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes

P(4) = 1/6

Final Answer: 1/6
